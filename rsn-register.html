📖 How to use this dashboard

1
Scan Market

Click 🔬 Scan Market to pull the latest data for all Indian stocks. Qualified results appear in the table below.

2
Pick a timeframe

Choose Daily, Weekly or Monthly — how long-term you want the trend to be.

3
Choose the EMA lens

Auto lets the app detect the exact EMA a stock respects. Or pick a fixed one (11 / 21 / 51 / 101 / 201).

4
Sector Rotation

Sectors are ranked by momentum + strength vs Nifty. The number on each chip = how many scanned stocks are in that sector. Click a sector to filter the scan.

5
Filter & search

Use ⚙ Filters for NSE / BSE, and the search box to find any stock instantly.

6
Open a stock

Click any row to see its chart — candles, EMA, timeframe and price. Score 70+ and Never Broke = a strong EMA follower.

7
Share a chart

In the chart popup, use ⬇ Download or 📋 Copy Chart — the image is branded and ready to share anywhere.

8
Make it yours

Open ⚙ Settings to change the dashboard theme, accent colour, chart candle colours, chart background and EMA line colour.
